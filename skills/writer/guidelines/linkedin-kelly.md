# Guideline: LinkedIn (Professional & Visionary)

**Target Audience:** Founders, operations leaders, managers, and B2B professionals.
**Goal:** Establish thought leadership, showcase system-level thinking, and build professional trust.

## Core Philosophy (The Shift in Perspective)
LinkedIn readers do not want to read a diary entry or a list of complaints about how hard work is. They want actionable insights, decision-making frameworks, and scalable systems.
- **From "Executioner" to "Architect"**: Always frame the narrative around transitioning from a manual worker to a system operator or commander.
- **From "Saving Time" to "Removing Cognitive Load"**: Don't just say "we saved 3 hours." Say "we removed the cognitive load of fragmented attention."
- **Systems > Hacks**: Focus on *how* the system was built and *why* it scales, not just the quick fix.

## Structural Rules (The Hook-Driven Format)
1. **The Hook (First 1-2 lines)**: Start with a strong, counter-intuitive statement or a highly relatable pain point. Reject clickbait, but be punchy.
   *Example: "Stop Treating Your Best Salespeople Like Data Entry Clerks."*
2. **The "Why" Before the "How"**: Before detailing the solution, clearly define the business or operational bottleneck. Frame it as an organizational problem, not just a personal one.
3. **Structured Breakdowns**: Use bold subheadings and bullet points. LinkedIn readers skim.
4. **The Takeaway / Lesson**: End with a philosophical or strategic shift (e.g., "AI won't replace your creative vision, but it will replace your drudgery.").
5. **The CTA**: Keep it soft and discussion-oriented. Ask a question to drive comments, or point to a broader resource (like `buda.im`) rather than hard-selling a specific feature.

## Formatting & Aesthetics
- **White Space is King**: Keep paragraphs strictly to 1-3 lines. Never write blocks of text.
- **Visuals**: Use authentic, professional images (e.g., real event photos, clean workflow diagrams, system screenshots). **Red Line: No Chinese text in screenshots unless targeting a purely domestic audience. No QR codes.** Use English versions of UI/diagrams whenever possible.

## Tone & Vocabulary (Professional Polish)
- **Use Business English**: Use terms like *Operational Bottleneck*, *Cognitive Load*, *System Architecture*, *Fragmented Attention*, *ROI*.
- **Vulnerability is Good**: It is okay to share failures or "messy" realities (e.g., "our live demo failed"), as it builds authenticity and trust among peers.
- **Avoid Slang & Emojis**: Keep emojis to an absolute minimum (0-2 per post). Do not use casual internet slang.

## Anti-Patterns (What NOT to do)
- Do NOT use a "diary" tone ("Today I was so tired because...").
- Do NOT use overly emotional exclamations ("This is mind-blowing!!!").
- Do NOT post raw chat transcripts without structuring the takeaway.
- Do NOT end with "Follow me for more tips." Instead, end with a thought-provoking question or a link to a system/tool.
